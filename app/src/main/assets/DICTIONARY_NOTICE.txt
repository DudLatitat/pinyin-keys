en_words.txt is derived from en_US_wordlist.combined.gz in the Android Open Source
Project's LatinIME (platform/packages/inputmethods/LatinIME).
Copyright (c) 2008, The Android Open Source Project
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
Changes: reformatted, offensive/not-a-word entries removed, limited to the 80,000 most
common words (see tools/build_english_dict.py).

en_phrases.txt is derived from English sentences in the Tatoeba collection
(https://tatoeba.org), licensed under CC BY 2.0 FR
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). Changes: reduced to the three most
common next words after common one- and two-word contexts (see tools/build_phrase_table.py).

emoji_names.tsv: emoji names and keywords from emojibase-data 15.3.2
(https://github.com/milesj/emojibase), MIT licence, derived from Unicode CLDR annotations.

en_words.txt frequencies: wordfreq 3.1.1 (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) by Robyn Speer,
data licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The word
list itself is from LatinIME (above); built with tools/build_english_freqs.py.

en_phrases.txt: next-word counts from Tatoeba English sentences (https://tatoeba.org),
CC BY 2.0 FR; built with tools/build_phrase_table.py.

en_lm.bin: counts of 1-, 2- and 3-word sequences from Tatoeba English sentences
(https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR; built with tools/build_ngram_lm.py.
